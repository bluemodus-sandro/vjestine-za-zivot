# VJEŠTINE ZA ŽIVOT
## Three-book production and educational-edition blueprint

Research date: 22 September 2026. Proposed project start: 28 September 2026.

This package is a production plan, not a set of finished manuscripts, approved school materials, legal advice or ready-to-use technical/clinical instructions. All production tasks are NOT STARTED. Owners are roles to hire/assign, not people already engaged. No institution has endorsed this project.

## The series

| Book | Working subtitle | School band | Reading approach | Planned pages | Indicative trade launch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mali koraci, velike vještine | OŠ 1-4 | Co-read in grades 1-2; independent-reading design centred on grades 3-4 | 192 | June 2027 |
| 2 | Mogu više! | OŠ 5-8 | Extra scaffolding for grade 5; growing independence in grades 6-8 | 224 | February 2028 |
| 3 | Spremni za samostalan život | SŠ 1-4, including three-year routes | Teen/adult-transition scenarios; legal eligibility and professional supervision kept explicit | 256 | October 2028 |

All three books contain 60 skill chapters in 10 modules, plus 10 four-page integrated missions. The purpose is not to make every child do everything alone. The common promise is: **Znam probati. Znam provjeriti. Znam kada tražiti pomoć.**

The progression is deliberate: recognise and practise with support -> plan and perform appropriately -> compare, manage and navigate adult systems. Money grows from euros and needs/wants to pocket-money budgets, then payslips, credit, savings and household cash flow. Cooking moves from cold breakfasts to tested hot meals and meal planning. Home skills move from caring for belongings to maintenance, renting and shared bills. Mobility moves from adult-led journeys to route planning, vehicle costs and professionally supervised maintenance awareness.

The three proposed covers use one grid, trim, typography system, icon family and aligned spine numbers, with teal/ochre/rust age-band accents. Illustration maturity and text density increase with the reader. These are design specifications, not completed cover artwork. All titles and brand choices remain subject to clearance and reader testing.

Start with 01 and filter by **Planning_Week_Start <= 4** for the first month. Filter by **Applies_To** for B1, B2, B3 or Series. Filter **Priority = P0** for critical review/release gates. P1 means core planned work; P2 means an optional expansion or early school-edition branch, not a promise to produce it.

Chapter IDs such as B3-028 stay fixed even if chapter order changes. B3-028-W01 is a worksheet, -C01 a card, -TG teacher notes, -PG parent notes and -Q01 optional self-check content. These IDs identify planned assets, not files already included. B3-M05-MISSION identifies the fifth module's integrated mission. CM-### references rows in the curriculum file; S## references the source register.

CSV format: UTF-8 with BOM, comma delimiter, CRLF records and properly quoted fields. Croatian diacritics are preserved. Import IDs as text. In a Croatian-locale spreadsheet application, use its text/CSV import and explicitly choose UTF-8 and comma if double-click opening puts everything in one column. Semicolons inside a cell separate IDs or tags; they are not the CSV delimiter. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD. CSV cannot preserve colour, filters, formulas or column widths; turn on wrapping and filters after import. No macros or executable spreadsheet formulas are included.

Planning instructions are in English; book/module/chapter and reader-facing label proposals are in Croatian. This is intentional: it is a working production brief, not the final Croatian manuscript. A Croatian editor must review the final prose.

## What to make first

The initial funded deliverable is a **nine-chapter prototype plus three coordinated cover/spine concepts**, not 180 finished chapters or an app. The sample chapters are B1-016/020/032, B2-017/019/044 and B3-002/013/047. Money, food, communication and information-checking test both the practical promise and the visual age progression.

During the first month, agree the cash/time ceiling and product promise; interview adult buyers/educators; commission legal and curriculum discovery; get comparable editorial/illustration/print quotes; select specialists; and prototype the cover/spread system. The first child-facing trials occur only after professional review, safeguarding/privacy arrangements and applicable permissions.

A proposed pilot target is 12 readers per age band and eight educator reviewers, with accessible participation. This is a small usability sample, not representative market research or proof of learning effectiveness. A proposed internal usability threshold is 80% completing at least two of three key checks with the agreed age-appropriate support. Do not turn that threshold into a public efficacy claim.

## Stage the product line

At each trade launch: book, parent/caregiver guide, worksheets and answer notes, printable 72-card deck, private skills passport and essential accessible web extras. A teacher-guide draft may exist, but formal school use/distribution must follow the applicable route. A physical deck is not ordered until demand and manufacturing economics justify it.

Each deck contains 60 skill cards, 10 mission cards and two instructions cards. Each teacher-guide target is approximately 96 pages: 60 lesson briefs plus preparation, differentiation, curriculum mapping, a 12-lesson starter path and a proposed 35-session club path. The 35-session option is not a claim to replace CARNET's official AI curriculum. The parent-guide target is about 24 pages with a topic coverage index.

Audio, specialist accessible formats, a small demonstration-video library, facilitator training, classroom kits, translations and a slipcase set are staged expansions. The future app has an explicit demand/privacy/operating-cost gate; there is no child-facing open-ended medical, legal or repair chatbot in the base product. No child behavioural advertising is planned.

## Page budget and reuse rules

Common allowance in each book: 20 pages for ten module openings, 40 for ten missions and 12 for front/back matter. Book 1 adds sixty two-page skill chapters: 192 pages. Book 2 has 44 two-page and 16 four-page chapters: 224 pages. Book 3 has 28 two-page and 32 four-page chapters: 256 pages. The exact complex chapters are identified in Core_Pages. A changed trim or signature count must be approved across the set before final layout.

The four-page missions are additional integrated applications, not duplicate chapter-page counts. Chapter 60 teaches the capstone planning approach; the tenth mission applies it to a new scenario. Readers can use separate paper rather than write irreversibly in the trade book. Print-at-home sheets are optional conveniences, with oral/notebook alternatives.

## Curriculum alignment: what is and is not finished

Every topic has proposed theme tags: POD (Poduzetništvo), OSR (Osobni i socijalni razvoj), UKU (Učiti kako učiti), IKT (Uporaba IKT-a), ODR (Održivi razvoj), GOO (Građanski odgoj i obrazovanje), and ZDR (Zdravlje). The Ministry's official sources and 2025 implementation guidance are S01-S08.

The 55 mapping rows contain official codes located in sources and our proposed relevance to selected lessons. They are **not** 55 approved lessons, not a complete national-curriculum coverage claim, and not a substitute for exact-grade teacher review. Blank Curriculum_Map_IDs mean that no code-level anchor has been attached yet; theme tagging remains only a starting hypothesis. Some mapped activities cover only part of an expectation; the Coverage_Limitation field makes this explicit. No official code was invented to fill a gap.

Book boundaries differ from curriculum cycles. In the general cross-curricular structure, cycle 2 crosses the book boundary because it includes grades 3-5. High-school mapping must account for three-year versus four-year routes and official combined codes such as uku A.4/5.1. Teacher-guide production must resolve each actual grade and pathway.

Praktične vještine anchors are specifically from an experimental curriculum and are not applied to all schools or all ages. Special daily-living curricula have their own setting; inclusive mainstream design is not automatically a special-programme edition. Vocational SIU mapping must be qualification-specific.

## Policy opportunities and their limits

**Cross-curricular guidance and whole-day schooling:** use ready-to-run practical lessons and low preparation burden as the proposition. Official whole-day information describes the 62-school experiment through 2026/2027; it does not establish guaranteed universal 2027/2028 implementation or purchases of this series (S08-S11).

**Device rules:** 2026 primary-school restrictions have school-approved educational, health and other exceptions. The design therefore works fully offline, with optional teacher-controlled digital enrichment rather than mandatory phones, QR access or child accounts (S12, S27).

**AI literacy:** CARNET's 1 September 2026 publication completes curriculum coverage from primary grade 5 through high school; primary extracurricular and high-school optional routes differ. Its published participation call runs through 1 September 2029. This is a route to relevant lesson design, not a compulsory nationwide subject or publisher grant (S13).

**Vocational reform:** Book 3 can support selected transferable work/life skills, but a vocational-subject version may require ASOO rather than AZOO. ASOO publishes its own procedure and checklist. Obtain written classification; do not transfer AZOO's filing deadline or attachments to ASOO without verification (S14-S16, S34).

**Funding:** the verified annual 2026/2027 material-financing decision is limited to specified contexts; it is not a universal book subsidy. The post-2026 financial-literacy strategy remains a watch item, not an assumed new mandate. The 2027 literary-creation call closed on 14 September 2026, before this plan's research date, and eligibility for practical nonfiction is not assumed. Budget grant income and guaranteed school orders at zero (S21, S22, S32).

## Educational-edition route and timing

Trade publication, lawful evaluation, curriculum alignment, official approval/listing, school selection and actual funding are separate steps. Do not present one as proof of another. A natural person obtaining an ISBN is not the same question as an eligible applicant for commercial school materials or a compliant recurring retail business (S16, S24).

For the **AZOO route**, the published procedure states a filing deadline of 31 March for the following school year. Its 5 February 2024 amendment updates electronic delivery to USB and/or a link alongside the written submission. Confirm the current checklist, fees, classification and annual deadline in writing. The old CD wording must not be reused uncritically (S17-S19).

An optional standalone 12-lesson sampler can target the 2027/2028 window only if entirely ready and correctly classified; internal readiness is before 10 March 2027, with filing planned before 31 March. It must not delay the main trade book or become a backdoor unapproved classroom product. An incomplete sampler is deferred.

The mature B1 educational edition targets filing in March 2028 for 2028/2029. B2 and the general B3 school edition target a 2029/2030 route. B3 vocational variants follow the confirmed competent-agency timetable; March 2029 is a planning target, not a verified ASOO statutory deadline. Review duration and approval are not promised. School gifts, conflicts and sample/pilot arrangements need legal clearance before materials or benefits are offered (S15-S20, S34).

## Safety and child agency

L means low-risk paper or ordinary activity. A means an actively supervising responsible adult for the practical version. P means a qualified instructor/professional; a paper alternative always exists. These labels are editorial safeguards, not legal authorisations or universal age thresholds. Grade alone never proves competence.

B3-028 covers a dead battery and the decision to use a supervised 12 V jump-start, with 16+ editorial placement and a qualified technician. The exact vehicle and booster manuals govern any practical demonstration. There is no generic cable sequence, EV traction/high-voltage servicing, damaged/frozen/leaking battery activity or roadside reenactment. Vehicle lifting has comparable controlled-workshop limits. The plan is not itself a technical tutorial (S30).

First aid requires current qualified review and approved inert/mannequin practice; no CPR or choking manoeuvres on peers and no real practice emergency calls. Food chapters require tested recipes, allergy controls and current hygiene/storage review. Electrical, gas, structural and hazardous chemical work stays at recognise-stop-call scope. Clinical, legal and financial facts need exact current operational sources, not just curriculum references (S07, S28-S30, S33).

Use fictional money, health and household examples. Never require personal trauma, income, immigration status, passwords or intimate disclosures. Consent and privacy rules are context-specific; the plan includes professional review rather than assuming a consent form solves every issue (S27). Civic activities assess procedure, evidence and respectful reasoning, not political beliefs or party preferences.

## Schedule, budget and project-control assumptions

The dates are a proposed baseline with parallel contracted writers, editors, designers and reviewers, not a promise that one person can do every role. Effort ranges are planning allowances, not quotes. Resource-load them in STR-09 and move dates before committing externally. School trials depend on the actual local calendar and permission; summer content tests are family/community evaluations where appropriate. Late-December 2027 calendar space is left before January 2028 prepress. Print lead times and specialist availability override launch targets.

Normal task dependencies have been checked for missing IDs, cycles and predecessor date ordering. Long OPS tasks are recurring review windows, not hundreds of weeks of continuous labour. Legal deposit is event-triggered by actual printing: the operational goal is prompt dispatch within seven days, never exceeding the applicable 30-day obligation, irrespective of the planned retail launch (S26).

Get supplier quotes before fixing prices or print quantities. Evaluate 300/500/1000-copy scenarios rather than assuming the biggest run is best. Model each channel's net receipts, relevant taxes, discounts, payment fees, fulfilment, shipping, returns, damaged copies and deposit copies. Separate fixed editorial/art/review costs from variable costs. Break-even uses contribution per sold copy, not retail price. Confirm recurring business/tax/consumer duties with Croatian professionals. No grant or unsigned school order funds the base case.

## Completion standard and handoff

A task is done only when its named deliverable exists and Acceptance_Criteria are met; change Status to IN PROGRESS, BLOCKED, REVIEW or DONE as appropriate. Keep actual dates and quoted costs in new columns rather than overwriting historical decisions. A safety-critical text or image change reopens review of every linked asset. No unresolved critical risk passes a print, pilot or school-submission gate.

Give the author the applicable topic CSV and 11; the designer 06 and page budgets; the curriculum lead 07, 08 and 10; the producer 01, 05 and 09. The complete operational/clinical/legal fact-check bibliography will grow during authoring. The 34 seed sources verify this plan's framework; they do not mean every future chapter is already fact-checked.

The first commercial decision is whether a tested, attractive, useful Book 1 deserves production investment. Books 2 and 3 share the system but each must earn its own production budget. The strategy is **design the series now, validate a small sample, publish sequentially, and expand only on evidence**.
